The Daiquiri is a calibration tool. Three ingredients, one of them the rum, and
nothing in the glass that can disguise a bad one — which is why bartenders order
it to find out what a bar is like. If the Daiquiri is right, the lime was
squeezed today and someone is paying attention.

Its reputation was damaged by a machine. The frozen, neon, pre-mixed version
shares a name and almost nothing else with the shaken drink, and a great many
people who believe they dislike Daiquiris have only met that one.

Balance is personal here in a way it is not elsewhere. The spec above is a
starting point; the sugar is the dial, and moving it a quarter ounce in either
direction is not a mistake, it is the adjustment the drink is designed for.
