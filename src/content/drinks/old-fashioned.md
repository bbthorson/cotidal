This is the control sample. Every other cocktail is a modification of it, and
the ones that wandered furthest are the reason it needed a name at all.

What makes it difficult is that there is nowhere to hide. Four components, one
of them water, and the water is the one most often got wrong — an Old Fashioned
stirred for eight seconds is a different and worse drink than the same
ingredients stirred for thirty. Dilution is an ingredient here, which is the
whole argument for stirring it rather than building it in the glass and hoping.

The muddled orange slice and the cherry are a mid-century addition. Nobody has
to take them out, but they are answering a question the original does not ask.
