The Toronto is an exercise in restraint with one of the most volatile ingredients in
the world: Fernet-Branca.

Fernet's intense menthol, gentian, and myrrh can easily bulldoze an entire cocktail.
In the Toronto, however, it is restricted to a quarter-ounce, functioning not as a base
spirit but as a mega-bitters modifier.

Rye or robust Canadian whisky provides dry grain and oak structure. A modest barspoon
of rich syrup softens Fernet's severe bitterness, allowing the herbal undertones of
saffron, rhubarb, and chamomile to bloom against the whiskey's caramel. Stirred ice-cold
with an expressed orange peel, it is brooding, complex, and intensely civilized.
