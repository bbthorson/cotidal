Lemon against sugar, three to two, so this is a properly sharp sour; the egg
white is what keeps it from tasting it. Shaken hard, it gives a thick, quiet
foam that softens the acid and carries the bitters, which go on top for their
smell, not stirred in for their taste.

That softness is why it sits richer than a [Daiquiri](/drinks/daiquiri) and
nearer the middle of the map: the spirit is grape, floral and unaged, but the
texture is closer to a [Clover Club](/drinks/clover-club).
