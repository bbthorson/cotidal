Tawny Port is time made liquid. While Ruby and Vintage Ports are aged with minimal oxygen
to preserve fresh blackberry fruit and fierce tannins, Tawny Port is deliberately exposed
to air over decades in seasoned wooden casks known as pipes.

In the porous oak, the wine breathes. Deep purple anthocyanins drop out of solution,
turning the wine from black-red to pale, golden amber. Primary fruit notes of plum and
cassis transmute into secondary and tertiary wonders: dried figs, orange peel marmalade,
toasted walnuts, cinnamon, and burnt sugar.

Fortified with neutral grape spirit (*aguardente*) to preserve natural residual sugar,
a 10- or 20-Year Tawny Port is silk in the mouth. It warms the chest without biting,
making it the definitive slow nightcap beside dark chocolate or Stilton cheese.
