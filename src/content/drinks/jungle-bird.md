The Jungle Bird is the bridge between Polynesian pop and Northern Italian aperitivo.
Until Jeffrey Ong dropped Campari into pineapple juice in Kuala Lumpur in 1973, tropical
tiki drinks had largely ignored bitter liqueurs in favor of spices, citrus, and falernum.

The stroke of genius is the pairing of Campari with pineapple juice. Fresh pineapple
juice contains the enzyme bromelain, which foams vigorously when shaken, creating a
creamy, frothy head that suspends Campari's intense gentian bitterness.

Blackstrap or heavy pot-still Jamaican rum provides earthy treacle, molasses, and
wood smoke that match Campari's weight. The result is sweet, savory, bitter, and tart
all at once—a tropical drink for drinkers who thought they hated tiki.
