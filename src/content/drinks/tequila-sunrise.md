The Tequila Sunrise is 1970s California captured in a highball glass. Its signature visual
effect—a gradient rising from deep crimson at the base through glowing gold to bright
yellow at the rim—relies entirely on fluid dynamics.

Grenadine has a much higher specific gravity than orange juice and alcohol. When poured
gently down the inner glass wall, it plunges directly to the bottom without mixing,
slowly bleeding upward into the citrus.

The drink's reputation suffered when made with artificial corn syrup grenadine and canned
juice. Made with pure pomegranate grenadine, freshly squeezed orange juice, and peppery
100% agave blanco tequila, it is vibrant, tart, and deeply nostalgic—a sweet-tart citrus
highball that genuinely looks like dawn breaking over the Pacific.
