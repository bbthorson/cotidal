Half the glass is orange juice, which is why the Mimosa sits so far up the
sweet side of the map: the wine is there for its bubbles and its edge more than
for anything it tastes of. Fresh juice matters more than the bottle. Juice from a
carton brings a cooked sweetness the wine can’t lift.

It sits well to the crisp side for the same reason a [Bellini](/drinks/bellini)
does: cold, sparkling and acid, with nothing in it that coats the mouth.
