Fino Sherry is wine pushed to its biological limit. Made from the neutral Palomino Fino
grape grown in blazing white *albariza* chalk soils, it is fortified to 15% ABV and aged
in soleras under a living blanket of yeast called *flor*.

The flor is miraculous. It feeds on oxygen, alcohol, and whatever residual glycerol and
sugar remain, shielding the wine from oxidation while pumping it full of acetaldehydes.
The result is absolute dryness: zero grams of sugar, zero unctuousness, and a sensation
on the tongue like crushed sea shells, raw almonds, baker's yeast, and Atlantic brine.

It must be treated like fresh white wine. Keep it in the refrigerator, pour it ice-cold,
and finish the bottle within a few days of opening alongside cured Iberian ham, salted
Marcona almonds, or fresh olives.
