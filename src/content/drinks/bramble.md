Dick Bradsell single-handedly pulled British bartending out of the dark ages of premixes
and blue Curaçao in the 1980s. Alongside his Espresso Martini, the Bramble was his masterpiece.

It is at heart a classic gin sour, but served over crushed ice and crowned with a slow,
luminous drizzle of French blackberry liqueur (*crème de mûre*). As the dark purple
liqueur weaves through the crushed ice pellets, it bleeds downward, creating a gradient
from tart lemon-juniper at the bottom to rich berry sweetness on top.

Crushed ice is non-negotiable. It chills the drink instantly and controls dilution,
softening the high-proof gin and sharp lemon acid as the drinker works through the glass.
