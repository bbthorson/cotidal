Phil Ward is cocktail history's foremost pioneer in bringing artisanal mezcal into modern
mixology. At Mayahuel, he treated mezcal not as a novelty or tequila substitute, but as a
complex, terroir-driven spirit on par with Islay Scotch or vintage Cognac.

The Division Bell reinterprets the Last Word matrix. By pairing mezcal with Aperol, Ward
softened the bitter punch, while Maraschino liqueur provides bone-dry cherry-stone
funk. Fresh lime gives tart acid, and an expressed grapefruit peel on the surface highlights
the vegetal smoke of the agave.

Tighter and drier than the Naked & Famous, the Division Bell leans savory, smokey, and
crisply refreshing.
