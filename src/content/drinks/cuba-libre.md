A rum and Coke with lime, which sounds like a technicality until you taste the
two side by side. Cola is sweet and dark — caramel, vanilla, a little spice —
and on its own it flattens the rum into sugar. The lime gives it an edge to
push against.

That is why it sits high on the sweet side of the map and only a little to the
crisp: the cola is most of the glass, and the lime and the bubbles are what
keep it from sitting with the dessert drinks.
