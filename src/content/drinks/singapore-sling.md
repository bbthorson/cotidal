Mostly pineapple, with three liqueurs layered over the gin: cherry, orange and
Bénédictine’s honey and herbs. The lime is small against all that fruit, so it
drinks sweet, and the herbs and the cherry give it a darker middle than a
plain fruit punch.

It sits just to the rich side of the middle and high on the sweet. Compare the
[Jungle Bird](/drinks/jungle-bird), another long pineapple drink, which has
Campari where this has cherry.
