Phil Ward's Oaxaca Old Fashioned was the Trojan horse of the 21st-century cocktail
revival. In 2007, most bar patrons were wary of artisanal mezcal, perceiving it as harsh
or primitive.

Ward's genius was the split base. Reposado tequila provides a gentle, oak-kissed foundation
reminiscent of bourbon, while a half-ounce of smoky Oaxacan mezcal lifts the profile into
the clouds. Agave nectar replaces refined sugar, mirroring the vegetal core of the spirits,
and Angostura bitters anchor the drink in familiar spice.

The flamed orange peel on top is crucial: burning the expressed peel caramelizes its
citrus oils, creating a sweet, smoky fragrance that sets up the palate for the rich,
golden, and savory liquid underneath.
