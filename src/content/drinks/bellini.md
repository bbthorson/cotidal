Purée, not juice, so the peach brings body as well as sugar. That puts the
Bellini a little richer and sweeter than a [Mimosa](/drinks/mimosa), though
still well to the crisp side: the base is cold Prosecco.

The IBA builds it in a mixing glass with ice rather than in the flute, which
chills the purée and mixes it without knocking out the bubbles. The variations
change the fruit and keep everything else.
