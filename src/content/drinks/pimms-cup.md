The Pimm's Cup belongs to the English 19th-century tradition of 'fruit cups'—low-proof,
herb-infused wine and spirit concoctions intended for daytime garden parties, cricket
matches, and boat races.

Pimm's No. 1 is gin-based, tinted tawny brown with bitter gentian, tea herbs, and sweet
spices. Its botanical profile leans savory and bittersweet, which makes cucumber slice
its essential aromatic counterpart: cucumber's cool vegetal smell cuts through the
caramelized sweetness of English lemonade or ginger ale.

The result is light, fragrant, and designed for stamina. It is as much an edible garden
as a drink, providing refreshing low-alcohol dilution under direct summer sun.
