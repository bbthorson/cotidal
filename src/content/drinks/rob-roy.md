The Rob Roy is the Manhattan transported across the Atlantic. While rye whiskey gives
the Manhattan spicy, sharp grain edges and bourbon rounds it with sweet corn, blended
Scotch introduces malted barley, heather, and a distant whisper of peat smoke.

The interplay between Scotch and Italian vermouth is richer and softer than its rye
counterpart. The herbal botanicals and vanilla of sweet vermouth latch onto the toasted
oak and honeyed cereal of the whisky, creating a darker, woodier profile.

A high-malt blended Scotch provides the necessary structure to stand up to sweet vermouth
without getting lost. Stirred until glistening and ice-cold, it is warming, elegant,
and historic.
