Where the Bamboo pairs sherry with dry vermouth for a crisp, saline finish, the Adonis
pairs sherry with sweet Italian vermouth for roundness, dried fruit, and spice.

The choice of sherry alters the drink completely. A pale, biological Fino yields a
vibrant, taut drink where saline yeast contrasts directly against the sweet, vanilla-and-herb
plushness of the vermouth. An oxidative Amontillado pushes the drink toward a Manhattan:
hazelnuts, polished wood, orange peel, and dried fig.

Orange bitters are essential to brighten the mid-palate and echo the citrus oils in the
vermouth. Low in alcohol, rich in aroma, and effortlessly smooth.
