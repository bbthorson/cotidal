Forget the fruit-bomb Australian Shiraz with sweet vanilla and jam. Northern Rhône Syrah
is severe, brooding, and intensely savory.

Grown on vertigo-inducing granite terraces that bake under the southern sun and are
swept by the freezing Mistral wind, the grape produces an ink-dark, tannic powerhouse.
Its chemical hallmark is *rotundone*—the identical organic compound that gives freshly
cracked black peppercorns their pungent heat.

Pouring a Northern Rhône Syrah (from Cornas, Hermitage, or Saint-Joseph) fills the room
with savory, animal aromas: cured charcuterie, woodsmoke, black olive tapenade, violets,
and black peppercorn. High acid and chalky, firm tannins frame the dark brambly fruit,
making it the greatest partner in all of wine for roasted lamb, game, and black truffles.
