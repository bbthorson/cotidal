Moscato d'Asti is joy in a glass. Unlike dense dessert wines or heavy liqueurs, it is
light as spun silk, clocking in at an airy 5% ABV and fizzing with tiny, delicate bubbles
(*frizzante*).

Moscato Bianco is uniquely expressive: it is one of the few grapes that yields a wine
smelling unmistakably like fresh grapes. The aroma billows from the glass in waves of
white peach, orange blossom, honeysuckle, and candied ginger.

Because fermentation is halted early in temperature-controlled tanks, the sweetness is
pure, unadulterated grape juice, kept crisp and light by lively carbonation and natural
grape acidity. Served ice-cold at brunch, on a sunlit afternoon terrace, or alongside
fresh fruit tarts.
