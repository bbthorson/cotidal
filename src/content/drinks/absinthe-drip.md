The Absinthe Drip is less a drink than an optical and physical metamorphosis. High-proof
French absinthe (traditionally *verte*, colored with petite wormwood and hyssop) begins
as a brilliant, emerald-green tincture.

When ice water is dripped drop-by-drop through a sugar cube on a perforated spoon, the
liquid transforms. The 'louche' is pure physics: anethole—the essential oil in anise
and fennel—is soluble in high alcohol but insoluble in water. As dilution passes the
threshold, the oils spontaneously precipitate into microscopic droplets, turning the
emerald spirit into a swirling, milky, opalescent pastel cloud.

The cold water unlocks volatile aromatics: bitter grand wormwood (*Artemisia absinthium*),
sweet green anise, alpine hyssop, and lemon balm. Never light it on fire; treat it with
patience, cold ice water, and reverence.
