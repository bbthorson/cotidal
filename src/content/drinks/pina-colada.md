Coconut cream is fat and sugar, and it is what puts the Piña Colada so far
over to the rich side: it coats the mouth the way a dessert does. Pineapple adds
sweetness and a little acid, and the IBA lists lime only as a historical touch.

Blending with ice thins it and keeps it cold, but that doesn’t make it crisp.
It sits at the top of the map’s sweet side, well over to the rich.
