One part cassis to nine of wine. That’s enough blackcurrant to colour the glass
and sweeten it, and not so much that the Champagne stops tasting dry. Pour the
cassis first and let the wine mix it, so there’s nothing to stir.

It sits as sweet as a [Mimosa](/drinks/mimosa) and a step less crisp: cassis
is a dense, dark liqueur, and it gives the wine weight as well as sugar.
