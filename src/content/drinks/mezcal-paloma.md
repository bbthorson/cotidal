The Mezcal Paloma takes Mexico's everyday cantina highball and elevates it into an earthy,
aromatic powerhouse. Tequila is clean and peppery; mezcal adds the deep resonance of
underground pit roasting—charred agave hearts, mesquite smoke, and wet river stones.

Grapefruit is the ultimate partner for smoke. The bitter naringin in pink grapefruit
echoes the char of the mezcal, while fresh lime juice adds crisp citric sharpness.

A rim of *sal de gusano* (sea salt blended with ground chile and toasted agave worms) or
smoked sea salt connects the nose to the liquid. As you sip, the mineral salt suppresses
excess bitterness, throwing the roasted sweetness of the agave into high relief.
