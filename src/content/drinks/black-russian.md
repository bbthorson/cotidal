Before the White Russian added cream, and decades before the Espresso Martini added
caffeine foam, the Black Russian was the undisputed king of after-dinner coffee drinks.

It is a pure 'duo' in cocktail taxonomy: a high-proof spirit sweetened and modified by
a single dense liqueur. Vodka provides ethanol power and a neutral, pristine canvas;
Mexican coffee liqueur brings dark roasted arabica beans, vanilla, and cane caramel.

Because there is no cream or citrus to distract the palate, the quality of the coffee
liqueur is transparent. Stirred cold over dense ice cubes, it is slick, bittersweet,
and potent—a sleek, dark nightcap with none of the confectionery clutter.
