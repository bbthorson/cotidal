The Moscow Mule was a masterpiece of mid-century industrial marketing that happened to
hit upon an irresistible thermodynamic trick.

Vodka provides almost no flavor of its own; it acts as a neutral canvas and proof
delivery vehicle for spicy ginger beer and fresh lime. The real genius is the copper mug.
Copper possesses extraordinary thermal conductivity: it instantly drops to the temperature
of the crushed ice inside, frosting the outside with thick white condensation and
delivering an intense sensation of sub-zero chill to the drinker's lips.

Spicy, fermented ginger beer with genuine ginger bite is essential. Sweet ginger ale
renders the drink flat and cloying; fiery ginger beer provides the pepper and bite that
vodka lacks.
