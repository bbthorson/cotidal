The 20th Century sounds on paper like an impossible collision: gin, aromatized wine,
lemon juice, and chocolate liqueur.

In execution, it is an astonishing revelation of balance. The secret is that white crème
de cacao is used not as a dessert sauce, but as a modifying sweetener. The cocoa notes
operate in the background, softening the citric sharpness of lemon juice and marrying
beautifully with the floral wine notes of Lillet Blanc and the piney juniper of dry gin.

The drink pours pale yellow and completely translucent. The palate experiences bright,
crisp citrus first, followed by a delicate, haunting wave of white chocolate and cocoa
butter on the finish. Streamlined, sleek, and fast, just like its namesake train.
