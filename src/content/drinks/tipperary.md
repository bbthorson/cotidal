The Tipperary is the Irish whiskey equivalent of the Bijou: a complex, high-proof meeting
of malt, fortified wine, and monastery herbs.

Ensslin's original 1916 spec called for equal parts, which modern palates find overwhelmed
by the sugar and 110-proof strength of Green Chartreuse. Modern craft practice bumps the
Irish whiskey to a dominant 1.5 or 2 ounces, reducing the Chartreuse to a supporting half-ounce.

Pot-still Irish whiskey brings orchard fruit, toasted grain, and a lush, oily mouthfeel
that harmonizes effortlessly with the 130 botanicals of Chartreuse. Sweet vermouth rounds
the corners, and Angostura bitters provide warm baking spice. Rich, green-gold, and bracing.
