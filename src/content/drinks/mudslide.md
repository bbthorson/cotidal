The Mudslide is what happens when tropical beach bar improvisation meets late-20th-century
liqueur innovation. When Baileys Irish Cream arrived in the Caribbean in the mid-1970s,
it was only a matter of time before someone swapped it into a White Russian in place
of fresh cream.

The result is pure decadence. By replacing neutral cream with Irish whiskey-infused
dairy and cocoa, the Mudslide doubles down on sweetness, roast coffee, and alcohol proof.
Vodka provides the spinal fluid that prevents the blend of two sweet liqueurs from
becoming completely inert.

Whether served on the rocks as an unhurried nightcap or blended with ice and chocolate
syrup at a beach club, it sits at the absolute outer edge of the Rich and Sweet quadrant.
