German Riesling Kabinett is wine walking a razor's edge. Grown on nearly vertical
cliffs of Devonian slate plunging into the Mosel or Rhine, it achieves an architectural
miracle: intense flavor and sweetness carried on an impossibly light frame of only 8% alcohol.

The secret is the tension between sugar and acid. Riesling produces the highest natural
acidity of any noble grape; when a small cushion of natural grape sugar is left in the
wine, it does not taste syrupy. Instead, the sugar and electric acidity cancel each other
out, leaving the palate vibrating with green apple, white peach, lime blossom, and wet slate.

It is gossamer, refreshing, and endlessly drinkable. At table, its piercing acid and
gentle sweetness cut through spicy Thai curries, salty cured pork, and rich charcuterie.
