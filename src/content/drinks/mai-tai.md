The Mai Tai is not a rum punch; it is an elaborate daisy. There is no pineapple juice,
no orange juice, and no float of artificial red grenadine. Strip away decades of resort
degradation and what remains is an exacting sour: high-ester aged rum brightened by fresh
lime and sweetened with orange curaçao and nutty, floral orgeat.

The almond oil in the orgeat provides rich mouthfeel, while the curaçao contributes
candied orange peel and cognac notes. The rum blend is crucial: funk from Jamaican pot
stills gives muscle, while grassy rhum agricole or rich Demerara rum supplies backbone.

Crushed ice is mandatory. The drink demands rapid chilling and steady, controlled dilution
to tame the heavy rum ester load and sweet almond syrup into a refreshing, complex finish.
