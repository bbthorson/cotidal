Pedro Ximénez (PX) is not simply sweet wine; it is an extreme physical state of matter.
Harvested grapes are laid out on round esparto grass mats in the scorching Andalusian sun
until they lose half their moisture to evaporation, concentrating sugars, acids, and
solids into pure raisin essence.

When poured, it moves like liquid obsidian or engine oil, clinging to the bowl of the
copita and leaving dark, viscous tears. At over 400 grams of sugar per liter, it is
among the sweetest beverages on earth, yet it avoids cloying sickness through natural
grape acidity and the drying wood tannins of decades in American oak casks.

The flavor is an overwhelming explosion of dark dried fruits: sun-dried raisins, dates,
black Turkish figs, roasted coffee beans, carob, and black treacle. Poured neat as the
ultimate dessert, or drizzled directly over vanilla bean ice cream.
