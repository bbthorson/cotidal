The French 75 is a champagne sour. Taking carbonation from effervescent wine rather
than plain soda water fundamentally shifts the balance: dry sparkling wine adds yeast,
brioche, and malic acidity alongside effervescence.

The drink's strength is deceptively cloaked. The gin provides botanical aromatics,
the lemon cuts with bright citric bite, and the wine lengthens the finish without
dragging down the pace.

Served cold and dry. Sweet sparkling wine ruins it; Brut or Extra Brut Champagne
maintains the brisk, percussive snap that gave the drink its artillery name.
