Fernet-Branca is the ultimate boundary line in modern drinking. Dark as used motor oil
and intensely bitter, it refuses to ingratiate itself with sweetness. The initial shock
is pure menthol, spearmint, and eucalyptus vapor, followed immediately by an avalanche
of medicinal bitterness from gentian, aloe, and myrrh.

Beneath the bitterness lies extraordinary botanical complexity. Saffron adds an earthy,
leather tone; rhubarb and chamomile offer drying tannins; cardamom and galangal warm the
finish. One year of resting in Slovenian oak vats knits the 27 ingredients into a single,
impenetrable tincture.

Long treated as a secret handshake among professional bartenders, Fernet is also an
everyday democratic staple in Argentina, where it is poured generously over ice and
cut with Coca-Cola.
