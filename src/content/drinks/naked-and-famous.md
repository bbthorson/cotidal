The Naked & Famous proved that mezcal could star in an equal-parts sour without steamrolling
its companions. Where Green Chartreuse and gin form the bracing, high-proof bedrock of the
Last Word, the Naked & Famous pairs the softer, honeyed saffron notes of Yellow Chartreuse
with the bittersweet orange of Aperol.

Mezcal's roasted agave and earthen smoke provide an essential savory counterpoint to
the sweet herbal botanicals. Fresh lime juice supplies the sharp citric acid that binds
all four distinct elements into a tight, harmonious chord.

Its pale coral color glows in a crystal coupe. On the tongue, it begins bright and citrusy,
deepens into saffron and alpine honey, and finishes with lingering desert woodsmoke.
