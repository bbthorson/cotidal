The White Russian is the quintessential comfort cocktail: rich, milky, alcoholic, and
entirely unbothered by ascetic mixology rules.

Its structure is built on textural contrast. Heavy cream floated over cold coffee liqueur
and vodka creates a stark black-and-white visual divide in the glass. When un-stirred,
each sip draws cold, unsweetened velvet dairy first, followed immediately by sweet,
roasty coffee liqueur and proofed spirit.

Heavy cream is mandatory. Milk or half-and-half lacks the density to float cleanly and
curdles quickly against alcohol. Real heavy cream cushions the high proof of vodka,
turning what could be harsh ethanol into luxurious dessert.
