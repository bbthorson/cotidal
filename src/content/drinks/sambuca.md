Sambuca is Italy's most boisterous digestif. Viscous, crystal-clear, and intensely sweet,
it delivers an unapologetic blast of pure licorice and star anise oils.

The ritual of serving it *con la mosca* ('with the fly') is what elevates it from
ordinary cordials. Three dark, whole roasted espresso beans are floated on the surface
of the spirit. The drinker is meant to chew the roasted beans while swallowing the cold,
sugary liqueur.

The mechanics are brilliant: the bitter, dry, roasted crunch of the coffee bean provides
an immediate counterweight to Sambuca's syrupy sweetness, cutting the sugar and
highlighting the warming, medicinal spice of the anise.
