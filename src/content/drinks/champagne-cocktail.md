Sugar, bitters and a spirit is the old definition of a cocktail, and this is
that with Champagne for its water. The cube dissolves slowly, so the glass
sweetens as you drink it, and it keeps sending up a line of bubbles.

There is very little cognac, so it is mostly dry wine: crisp, and only just on
the sweet side of the map. The bitters are a seasoning, not a flavour you
notice as bitter.
