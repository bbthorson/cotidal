The Sea Breeze is an exercise in dual acidity. Where the Cape Codder pairs vodka simply
with cranberry, and the Greyhound pairs it with grapefruit, the Sea Breeze combines both
citrus and bog fruit into a single, vibrant highball.

The balance hinges on contrasting acid profiles: cranberry delivers sharp quinic and
malic acid with deep ruby color and drying fruit tannins, while grapefruit contributes
bright citric acid and bitter naringin.

Vodka provides clean alcoholic lift without competing with the fruit. On a warm day,
it is tart, refreshing, and clean, with a brisk, puckering finish that cleanses the palate.
