The Hugo Spritz is South Tyrol's answer to the orange-bitter hegemony of the Veneto.
Born in the alpine valleys of Alto Adige where German and Italian cultures meet, it
replaces the bitter gentian of Aperol with the ethereal, muscat-like perfume of
elderflower blossoms (*Holunderblüten*).

Where an Aperol Spritz demands bitter tension, the Hugo leans entirely on fresh,
verdant brightness: cold sparkling wine, fragrant mint, and lychee-like floral syrup.

The challenge is sugar management. Elderflower syrup or liqueur can easily skew cloying
if overdosed; high-acid Brut Prosecco, cold mineral soda, and fresh bruised mint leaves
are required to keep the finish crisp and dry.
