Sangria is a wine that has been turned into a punch: the fruit steeps in it,
the sugar rounds it, and ice and sometimes soda make it long. The wine stays
recognisable, but its tannin is mostly buried under fruit and sweetness.

That is why it sits with the sweet drinks rather than with the red wines on
the map. Steeping is the variable: a pitcher left overnight takes on more of
the citrus peel's bitterness, and a quick one stays brighter and sweeter.
