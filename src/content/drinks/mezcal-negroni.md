The classic Negroni relies on dry gin to provide a neutral, piney botanical lattice
for Campari and sweet vermouth. The Mezcal Negroni does something much bolder: it introduces
an intensely assertive spirit that rivals Campari's volume.

The smokiness of roasted agave binds directly with the bitter herbs of Campari and the
vanilla and caramel of Italian vermouth. Far from clashing, the combination tastes ancient
and intentional, like burnt orange peel, wild thyme, and dark cacao.

A rich vermouth with bitter undertones—such as Punt e Mes or Carpano Antica—holds up
best against the proof and woodsmoke of an espadín mezcal. Served over a single heavy
cube of ice to control dilution.
