A teaspoon of sugar to 60 ml of bourbon, so this is an
[Old Fashioned](/drinks/old-fashioned) in all but its ice and its herb.
Cracked ice dilutes it as it is stirred and keeps it very cold, and the mint is
mostly smell: muddled gently, so it gives up its oil and not its bitterness.

It sits rich and a little sweet, crisper than an Old Fashioned for the cold
and the mint, and sweeter for the bourbon’s vanilla.
