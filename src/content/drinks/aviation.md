The Aviation is one of the most volatile formulas in the cocktail canon. Done right,
it is pale periwinkle and tasting of citrus, pine, and wild alpine flora. Done with
a heavy hand, it tastes like grandmother's bath soap.

The danger lies in the crème de violette. Violette is intensely perfumed and sweet;
more than a barspoon or a scant quarter ounce will overwhelm both the dry juniper
of the gin and the nutty, funky cherry pit complexity of Maraschino.

The tartness of fresh lemon juice is the counterweight that keeps the two botanical
liqueurs tethered to the ground. Served ice cold in a crystal coupe with a dark
brandied cherry resting at the bottom like an anchor.
