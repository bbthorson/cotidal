The Millionaire is Gilded Age luxury preserved in amber. Its naming was deliberate:
at a time when most cocktails were simple combinations of whiskey, sugar, and bitters,
the Millionaire reached for the most expensive imported bottles on the backbar.

Grand Marnier—cognac blended with bitter Caribbean oranges—replaces basic citrus liqueurs,
infusing rich French brandy oak into the American bourbon base. A dash of absinthe
provides an electrifying lift of green anise and wormwood, while pomegranate grenadine
brings tart berry acid and deep amber-rose color.

The egg white is essential for binding these disparate, high-proof elements. Shaken hard,
it whips the mixture into a velvety froth, allowing bourbon heat, orange cognac, and
licorice aromatics to glide across the palate in rich, regal succession.
