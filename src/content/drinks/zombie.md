120 ml of rum, much of it dark and heavy, is what makes the Zombie rich and
strong; the lime, grapefruit and blended ice are what let it be drunk at all.
Falernum and cinnamon bring spice, and six drops of Pernod bring a smell of
anise you notice before you taste it.

It sits a little to the rich side and well up the sweet, beside the
[Mai Tai](/drinks/mai-tai) but richer, darker and spicier.
