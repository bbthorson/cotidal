Tommy's is the version that changed what the Margarita is for. Take out the
orange liqueur and the sugar stops being a flavour of its own; the agave nectar
echoes the spirit instead of competing with it, and a good tequila suddenly has
somewhere to be heard. A bad one has nowhere to hide, which is the trade.

It sits a step crisper than the [Margarita](/drinks/margarita) on the map for
the same reason: less sugar, nothing orange, more of the lime and the agave.

Salt on half the rim, and only half. It is a seasoning, not a border.
