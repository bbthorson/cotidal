The Bee's Knees is a study in how changing the sweetener changes the drink. Swap
white cane sugar for raw wildflower honey, and a standard gin sour is immediately
infused with meadow flowers, beeswax, and clover.

Honey syrup must be diluted slightly with warm water (typically a 3:1 ratio) before
shaking; pure honey seizes into an intractable cold lump upon contact with cocktail ice.

When shaken, honey proteins create a shimmering, golden emulsion and a creamy mouthfeel
that simple syrup cannot match. The citrus sharpness of fresh lemon cuts through the
viscous honey, while high-proof dry gin adds pine, coriander, and botanical backbone.
A radiant, golden sour.
