Passion fruit is both sour and perfumed, and here it comes twice, as purée and
as liqueur, with vanilla in the vodka and the sugar. The result is very sweet,
and the fruit’s acid is what keeps it drinkable.

The Champagne comes on the side, to drink between sips or pour in. Either way,
it is the crispest thing in the glass. On the map it sits high on the sweet
side, a little to the crisp.
