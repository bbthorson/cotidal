The Bamboo is a masterclass in elegance through subtraction. It contains no base distilled
spirit: no gin, no whiskey, no brandy. It is built entirely on two fortified wines:
bone-dry Andalusian sherry and botanical French dry vermouth.

Because neither ingredient exceeds 18% ABV, the drink sits at gentle aperitivo strength.
Yet there is nothing timid about it. Fino or Manzanilla sherry brings searing biological
dryness, yeast flor, almonds, and ocean brine. French vermouth adds wormwood, citrus peel,
and soft alpine herbs.

Angostura and orange bitters knit the two wines together, providing baking spice and
citrus oil. It is bone-dry, saline, complex, and extraordinarily civilized—the perfect
cocktail to begin an evening.
