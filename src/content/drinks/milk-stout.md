Milk Stout is a brilliant bit of biochemistry: brewer's yeast loves glucose, maltose,
and fructose, but it cannot touch lactose (milk sugar).

When lactose is added to the brewing kettle alongside dark roasted malts, it passes through
fermentation completely unmolested. While ordinary dry stouts finish bone-dry with roasted
bitterness, a Milk Stout retains smooth, non-fermented dairy sweetness and extra density.

The interplay is reminiscent of sweet iced coffee or dark chocolate with cream. Roasted
barley and black patent malt provide coffee roast, dark cacao, and ash; lactose and creamy
proteins cushion the tongue, softening the acrid edges into pure velvet.

Best enjoyed cellar-cool in a wide glass, delivering a rich, warming embrace that sits
comfortably in the Rich & Sweet quadrant.
