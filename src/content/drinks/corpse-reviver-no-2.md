The Corpse Reviver No. 2 is an exercise in four-way equilibrium. Four ingredients in
strict equal parts—gin, orange liqueur, aromatized wine, and lemon juice—locked in
balance, with an absinthe rinse providing an aromatic halo.

Because the original Kina Lillet carried a bitter quinine edge that modern Lillet Blanc
lacks, many contemporary bartenders substitute Cocchi Americano or add a drop of tonic
bitters to restore the intended bracing dry finish.

The absinthe must not be poured heavily into the shaker. A brief swirl around the cold
coupe coats the crystal surface, ensuring that anise and wormwood hit the senses as
vapor before the crisp, citrusy liquid arrives.
