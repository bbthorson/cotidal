The Paper Plane is arguably the most influential modern cocktail of the 21st century.
Like the Last Word before it, the formula rests on the deceptive simplicity of four
equal parts: bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and lemon juice.

The magic is in the interplay between the two bittersweet liqueurs. Aperol brings
bright orange zest and gentian bitterness; Amaro Nonino—distilled from whole grape
must and aged in Nevers oak—contributes alpine herbs, dried orange peel, and warm
caramel notes without heaviness.

Bourbon grounds the drink with corn sweetness and vanilla, while fresh lemon juice cuts
cleanly across the herbal sugars. Despite containing two assertive amari, the result is
luminous, accessible, and extraordinarily nimble on the palate.
