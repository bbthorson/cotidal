The Monte Carlo is what happens when you strip the Vieux Carré down to its bare essentials.
Remove the cognac, vermouth, and Peychaud's bitters, and you are left with a direct,
unsparing duel between spicy American rye whiskey and ancient French monastery liqueur.

Bénédictine is intensely sweet, but its sweetness is carried on a wave of saffron, mace,
cardamom, and angelica. Because of its density, a modest half-ounce is all that is required
to season two full ounces of 100-proof rye.

The rye's black pepper and charred oak bite through Bénédictine's honeyed weight, while
Angostura bitters anchor the mid-palate. Finished with an expressed lemon peel to lift
the aromatics, it is rich, austere, and deeply warming.
