The Rusty Nail belongs to the mid-century family of 'duos'—drinks built strictly on two
bottles, one spirit and one liqueur.

Its success depends on the botanical depth of Drambuie. Because Drambuie is itself made
from aged Scotch whisky infused with Scottish heather honey, anise, saffron, and alpine
herbs, it acts as an intensely rich, pre-seasoned simple syrup.

Pouring blended Scotch over Drambuie cuts the liqueur's syrupy density, reintroducing
dry oak, malt, and spirit proof. An expressed lemon twist is non-negotiable: the burst
of fresh citrus oil on the surface slices through the heavy honey coat, elevating the
drink from a sticky digestif into a sophisticated, brooding nightcap.
