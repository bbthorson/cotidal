The Whiskey Sour is the purest expression of the sour template: high-proof whiskey
tamed by fresh lemon juice and rounded by sugar. When balanced correctly, neither the
acid nor the grain dominates; they meet in a rich, citrus-flecked harmony.

Egg white is optional on paper, but essential for texture. When dry-shaken and then
aerated on ice, the proteins emulsify into a velvety microfoam that coats the tongue,
blunting the raw edge of lemon acid and whiskey heat without muting flavor.

Fresh lemon juice is non-negotiable. Commercial sour mix—a mid-century casualty of
convenience—replaced genuine citric acidity with processed syrup, single-handedly
cheapening one of the world's most disciplined classics.
