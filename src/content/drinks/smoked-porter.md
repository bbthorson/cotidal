Before the Industrial Revolution introduced coke-fired, indirect-heat kilns in the
eighteenth century, almost all beer on earth tasted of smoke. Green malt had to be dried
over open wood fires, infusing the grain with whatever timber was burning nearby.

Modern Smoked Porter resurrects that ancient union. When malt kilned over beechwood,
alder, or oak is married to dark roasted chocolate and black patent malts, something
magical occurs in the mash tun.

The acrid ash of the roast is softened by the sweet, savory woodsmoke phenols. On the
palate, it tastes of dark chocolate brownies, rich espresso roast, campfire smoke, and
charred bacon.

Cellar-cool in a wide snifter, it is thick, savory, warming, and comforting—the ultimate
cold-weather beer in the Rich & Savory quadrant.
