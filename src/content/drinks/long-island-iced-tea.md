Five spirits at 15 ml each is 75 ml of spirit, half as much again as most
highballs, and the lemon, syrup and cola are there to hide it. The cola is a
top, not a measure, so it is mostly a dark, sweet sour.

It sits near the middle of the map, a little to the crisp side: the lemon and
the ice keep it sharp, and the sugar keeps it high on the sweet side.
