While the international drinking world fixates on the Margarita, the Paloma is what
Mexico actually drinks. It is an unpretentious, thirst-quenching highball that leverages
the natural affinity between agave and bitter, sweet grapefruit oil.

The salt is not decorative. A pinch of salt in the liquid or on the lip of the glass
actively suppresses grapefruit's bitter naringin while accentuating the herbal, earthy
sweetness of agave.

Fresh lime juice provides sharp citric acidity to counterbalance the carbonated soda.
Whether made with Mexican Squirt, Jarritos Toronja, or fresh pink grapefruit juice cut
with soda water, the hallmark is crisp, effervescent refreshment with an undercurrent of
peppery tequila.
