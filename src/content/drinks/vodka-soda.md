The Vodka Soda is an anti-cocktail: no botanical complexity, no sugar, no aromatized
wine, and no fruit puree. It is pure functional drinking, designed for hydration,
calorie minimization, and uncomplicated alcoholic effect.

Because it offers nowhere to hide, its quality is entirely physical rather than culinary.
A poorly made Vodka Soda—flat tap soda from a soda gun over wet, hollow machine ice—is
bleak and chemical.

A well-executed one is a masterclass in carbonation physics: high-proof neutral spirit
shivering over dense, clear ice cubes, topped with vigorously carbonated, freezing cold
mineral water, and finished with a sharp burst of fresh lime peel oil across the surface.
Dry, crisp, and refreshing.
