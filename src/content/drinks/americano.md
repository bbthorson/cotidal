The Americano is where the bitter cocktail found its modern proportions. It unites
Lombardy and Piedmont in one glass: Campari from Milan (bitter herbs, bitter orange,
gentian) and sweet red vermouth from Turin (aromatized wine, wormwood, vanilla).

Without soda, the mixture is the dense, syrupy Milano-Torino. The addition of ice-cold
carbonated water unlocks the botanicals, releasing aromas of orange peel and bitter roots
while dropping the alcohol down to gentle session strength.

It is dry, restorative, and profoundly refreshing. The citrus oils expressed from an
orange wheel and lemon peel provide the top note that links the herbal bitterness of
Campari with the rich, fortified wine base.
