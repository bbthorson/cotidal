More triple sec than lemon, so for all its name this is a sweet drink: candy
lemon rather than fresh. The vodka adds strength and nothing else, which leaves
the orange liqueur to do the talking.

It sits well to the crisp side of the [Cosmopolitan](/drinks/cosmopolitan), and
sweeter, because there is no cranberry to darken it. A sugared rim is common and isn’t in
the IBA’s specification.
