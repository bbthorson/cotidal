Three parts lemon to one of syrup, so it is tarter than a
[Tom Collins](/drinks/tom-collins), and a splash of soda rather than a top,
so it is shorter and stronger too. Served without ice, it is meant to be drunk
quickly, while it is cold and still fizzing.

That sharpness is why it sits far to the crisp side and only a little sweet.
