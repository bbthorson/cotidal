The IBA names the rum by brand, which is unusual: Goslings, a dark, heavy
Bermudian rum. Its molasses is what moves this drink richer than a
[Moscow Mule](/drinks/moscow-mule), whose vodka gives the ginger nothing to
lean against.

The rum is floated, not stirred in, so the first sips are mostly rum and it
mixes as you drink. The lime is a garnish here, not a measure, which leaves the
drink sweeter than it looks.
