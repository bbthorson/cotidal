Ada Coleman was one of the most revered figures in bartending history, holding court
at the Savoy American Bar for over two decades. Her enduring legacy is the Hanky Panky.

At its core, the drink is a Sweet Martini: equal parts gin and sweet vermouth. But
Coleman's stroke of genius was adding two dashes of Fernet-Branca.

Two dashes—just enough to introduce menthol, bitter gentian, and myrrh without turning
the drink into cough medicine. The Fernet cuts straight through the syrupy sweetness of
Italian vermouth, drying out the finish and leaving the tongue tingling with bitter
herbs and juniper pine. An expressed orange peel on top supplies bright citrus oils.
Sophisticated, historic, and razor-sharp.
