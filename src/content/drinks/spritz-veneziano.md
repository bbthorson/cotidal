To order a 'Spritz' in Venice without specifying a brand will bring you a drink made
with Select, not Aperol. Formulated in the Castello district in 1920 with 30 botanicals—notably
juniper berry and rhubarb root—Select is darker, drier, and far more complex than its
sugary modern imitators.

The olive is essential. The green Castelvetrano olive resting on its pick is not an
afterthought; its saline brine cuts across the bitter herbal oils and fruit acid,
grounding the sparkling drink in savory lagoon soil.

While sparkling Prosecco is ubiquitous today, traditional bàcari often build the drink
on crisp, still white wine and high-pressure seltz, maintaining mineral clarity without
excessive foam.
