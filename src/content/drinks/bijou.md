The Bijou is a high-wire botanical act. With three assertive ingredients in equal parts—gin,
sweet vermouth, and Green Chartreuse—it would collapse in the hands of lesser ingredients.

The linchpin is Green Chartreuse. Clocking in at 55% ABV (110 proof) and infused with 130
alpine herbs by Carthusian monks, Chartreuse is intensely pungent: anise, peppermint,
thyme, and gentian. Paired with juniper-rich gin and the dried fruit and wormwood of sweet
vermouth, the combination creates a deep, kaleidoscopic harmony.

Stirred cold, it glows amber-green in the glass. The orange bitters bridge the botanical
herbs with citrus brightness, yielding an opulent, complex drink that argues back with every sip.
