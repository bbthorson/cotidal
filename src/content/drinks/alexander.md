To modern palates accustomed to brandy or rum in dairy drinks, the original Alexander
sounds like an anomaly: dry juniper gin paired with chocolate and heavy cream.

Yet in the glass, the botanical interaction is fascinating. Piney juniper, coriander,
and angelica root in London Dry gin cut directly through the sweet cocoa butter of white
crème de cacao. Rather than clashing, the gin imparts an alpine freshness that keeps the
cream tasting crisp and surprisingly light.

White crème de cacao is critical; dark cacao turns the drink muddy brown, whereas white
cacao yields a pristine, snowy ivory pour that explains why it was created to celebrate
a woman dressed in pure white.
