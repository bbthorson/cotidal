The Surf is the quarter of the chart where crisp meets sweet: away from
weight, toward sugar. It is a place on a map, not a style, and a drink is in it
because the chart sets it there.

Most of what is here is a sour — spirit, citrus, sugar — or a sour made long.
Short: the [Daiquiri](/drinks/daiquiri), the [Margarita](/drinks/margarita),
the [Caipirinha](/drinks/caipirinha), the [Bee's Knees](/drinks/bees-knees),
the [Southside](/drinks/southside), the [Whiskey Sour](/drinks/whiskey-sour).
Long: the [Tom Collins](/drinks/tom-collins), the [Mojito](/drinks/mojito), the
[Paloma](/drinks/paloma), the [Moscow Mule](/drinks/moscow-mule), and the
[French 75](/drinks/french-75), which lengthens with sparkling wine instead of
soda. The spritzes are here as well, the [Aperol Spritz](/drinks/aperol-spritz)
and the [Hugo](/drinks/hugo-spritz).

Three drinks put a bitter aperitivo into a sour — the
[Paper Plane](/drinks/paper-plane), the
[Naked and Famous](/drinks/naked-and-famous) and the
[Division Bell](/drinks/division-bell) — and the chart places all three close
to the centre. Of the wines,
[Moscato d'Asti](/drinks/moscato-d-asti) and
[Riesling Kabinett](/drinks/riesling-kabinett) land here.
