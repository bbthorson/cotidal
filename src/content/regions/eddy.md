The Eddy is the quarter of the chart where rich meets sweet: toward weight and
toward sugar. It is a place on a map, not a style, and a drink is in it because
the chart sets it there.

Cream and coffee account for much of it. The [Alexander](/drinks/alexander),
the [Brandy Alexander](/drinks/brandy-alexander), the
[Grasshopper](/drinks/grasshopper), the [White Russian](/drinks/white-russian)
and the [Mudslide](/drinks/mudslide) are spirit or liqueur folded into cream;
the [Espresso Martini](/drinks/espresso-martini) and the
[Irish Coffee](/drinks/irish-coffee) are built on coffee. The chart is precise
about the cream: the Black Russian, the same drink without it, is placed in The
Deep.

The rest are mostly sours made rounder. The [Sidecar](/drinks/sidecar) is here,
and so are the drinks that soften a sour with egg white or a liqueur — the
[Clover Club](/drinks/clover-club), the [Pink Lady](/drinks/pink-lady), the
[Millionaire](/drinks/millionaire), the [Amaretto Sour](/drinks/amaretto-sour).
And the sweet wines: [Sauternes](/drinks/sauternes),
[tawny port](/drinks/port-tawny), [Pedro Ximénez](/drinks/pedro-ximenez), with
[milk stout](/drinks/milk-stout) beside them.
