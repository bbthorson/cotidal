The Salt Line is the quarter of the chart where crisp meets savory: away from
sugar, and away from weight. Like the other three it is a place on a map, not a
tradition, and a drink is in it because the chart sets it there.

Most of what lands here is either cold and short or long and unsweetened. The
short drinks are spirit and dry vermouth with little else — the
[Dry Martini](/drinks/dry-martini), the [Gibson](/drinks/gibson), the
[50/50](/drinks/50-50-martini), the [Vesper](/drinks/vesper). The long ones
stretch a spirit with something sharp rather than sweet: tonic in the
[Gin and Tonic](/drinks/gin-and-tonic), lime and soda in the
[Rickey](/drinks/rickey), grapefruit in the [Greyhound](/drinks/greyhound).

Sherry and the aperitivo meet here too. The [Bamboo](/drinks/bamboo) and the
[Adonis](/drinks/adonis) are sherry with vermouth; the
[Americano](/drinks/americano) is Campari and sweet vermouth lengthened with
soda, and sits on this side of the chart while the Negroni — the same two
bottles with gin in place of the soda — sits in The Deep. Bottles are placed as
well as cocktails: [fino sherry](/drinks/sherry-fino),
[Sancerre](/drinks/sancerre), [Pilsner Urquell](/drinks/pilsner-urquell) and
[gose](/drinks/gose) are all on this line.
