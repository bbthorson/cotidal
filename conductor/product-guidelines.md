# Cotidal: Product Guidelines

## Brand Identity & Voice
- **Name:** Cotidal
- **Voice:** The AI Sommelier is **Witty & Sophisticated**. It speaks like a knowledgeable, slightly mystic friend who knows their way around a cellar but remains accessible and engaging.
- **Mystic Framing:** The experience is framed as a **"Reading"** or **"Revealing"** of the user's palate. We use thematic vocabulary like "Aura," "Essence," and "Vibration" to describe flavor profiles.

## Visual Language
- **Aesthetic:** **Organic & Mystic**. The UI should blend glassmorphism (frosted glass effects) with textures that evoke physical ingredients (herbs, liquid, botanical sketches).
- **Palette:** **Botanical & Earthy**. Muted greens (Sage), warm terracottas, and obsidian accents.
- **Atmosphere:** Subtle gradients and ethereal, celestial animations that suggest a "digital triangulation" occurring behind a mystical veil.

## Interaction Principles
- **Dynamic Tiered Calibration**:
    1. **The Threshold (Binary):** Start with low-friction, factual questions (e.g., "Coffee or Tea?") to establish the broad quadrant.
    2. **The Refinement (Tactile):** Use tactile sliders for downstream calibration (e.g., "Smokiness" or "Sweetness") based on the first set of answers.
    3. **The Revelation (Specifics):** Final confirmatory questions that choose between specific flavor profiles (e.g., "Licorice or Orange?").
- **Real-time Feedback:** As users interact with sliders, the "Palate Map" or visual "Aura" should pulse and shift to reflect their changing coordinate.

## Content & Education
- **In-Flow Wisdom:** Education is integrated into the recommendation explanations. The AI Sommelier explains *why* a drink matches, using both technical insight and mystic prose.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Technical terms (e.g., "Tannins," "Acid Tolerance") are supported by tooltips to keep the main interface focused on the "Reading."
