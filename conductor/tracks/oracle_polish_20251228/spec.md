# Track Spec: The Oracle's Touch: AI Personality & Mobile Polish

## Goal
Infuse the AI Sommelier with the "Witty & Sophisticated" mystic voice and ensure the application experience is seamless and magical on mobile devices.

## Requirements

### 1. AI Sommelier (The Oracle)
- **Persona:** Update the GenAI prompt to embody "The Oracle" – a witty, sophisticated, and slightly mystic guide.
- **Flair:** Use thematic vocabulary (Aura, Essence, Vibration) in drink explanations.
- **Logic:** Ensure the AI references the user's specific flavor profile (e.g., preference for Rich or Crisp) without mentioning technical terms like "coordinates."

### 2. Mobile Optimization
- **Responsive Map:** Ensure the "Palate Map" container scales gracefully on small screens.
- **Touch Targets:** Increase the effective hit area for MapPins and the UserPin to prevent frustration on mobile.
- **Adaptive UI:** Ensure the results list and context switcher (if used) are easy to navigate with a thumb on a 375px screen.
